Shawnee Parking Lot Striping FAQ

Do you stripe parking lots in Shawnee?

Yes. Shawnee is part of our regular service area. We stripe and paint parking lots for businesses, churches, schools, nonprofits, and property managers in Shawnee and nearby communities.

How much does parking lot striping cost in Shawnee?

Pricing is the same across our service area: $25 per space for the first 20 spaces, $20 for spaces 21-50, and $15 after 50, with a $400 minimum. New layouts add 75% and night or weekend work adds 25%. Use the calculator or text a photo for a firm quote.

Can you stripe at night or on weekends in Shawnee?

Yes. Night and weekend striping is available so your customers and tenants aren't disrupted. After-hours work carries a 25% premium.

What do you need to quote my Shawnee lot?

A photo or the address, the approximate number of spaces, and any ADA stalls, fire lanes, stencils, or curbs. Text it to 913-353-4327 or use the form below.
