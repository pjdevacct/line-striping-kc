ADA Parking Stalls for Kansas City & Johnson County Properties

Accessible parking isn't optional. Properly sized handicap stalls, van-accessible spaces, and access aisles have to meet federal accessibility standards, and getting them wrong can lead to complaints and costly violations. Line Striping KC paints ADA-compliant stalls and logos for businesses, churches, schools, and property managers throughout Kansas City and Johnson County.

Whether you're adding a stall, refreshing faded markings, or striping a whole lot, we handle the dimensions, the access aisles, and the International Symbol of Accessibility logo so your property stays accessible.

What's Included

  • Handicap stalls sized to accessibility standards
  • Van-accessible stalls and access aisles
  • International Symbol of Accessibility logos
  • Refreshing faded ADA markings as part of a re-stripe

When You Need It

  • You're opening or re-striping a lot
  • Your ADA markings have faded or are missing
  • You've received a complaint or want to head one off
  • You're changing a layout or adding spaces

How Much Does It Cost?

ADA spaces are $55 each in our price calculator, on top of your base striping. The number of accessible spaces a lot needs depends on its total size, and local rules can add requirements, so if you're not sure how many you need, tell us your space count and we'll point you in the right direction.

ADA Parking Stalls FAQ

How many ADA spaces does my lot need?

It depends on your lot's total number of spaces and on local requirements. Text us your space count and we'll help you work out what's typically required, then paint the stalls to spec.

Do you paint the accessibility logo?

Yes. We apply the International Symbol of Accessibility to each accessible stall.

Can you add ADA stalls to an existing lot?

Yes. Adding a stall can mean adjusting nearby spaces, and we'll work that into your quote.
