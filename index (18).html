Curb Painting for Kansas City & Johnson County Properties

Painted curbs tell drivers where they can and can't stop. Red, yellow, and white curbs mark loading zones, no-parking areas, and fire lanes and keep traffic moving safely around your property.

Line Striping KC paints curbs for retail centers, offices, schools, churches, and apartment properties throughout the KC metro. Color meanings can vary by city, so we follow your local requirements.

What's Included

  • Red, yellow, and white curb painting
  • Loading zones and no-parking areas
  • Fire lane curbs (see fire lane painting)
  • Refreshing faded or chipped curb paint

When You Need It

  • Curb paint has faded or worn away
  • You need to mark a loading zone or no-parking area
  • A fire lane needs clear marking
  • You're refreshing a property before new tenants or a sale

How Much Does It Cost?

Curb painting is quoted with your project, often alongside striping. Text us a photo and the approximate length of curb, and we'll send a price.
