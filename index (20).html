Parking Lot Layout Design for Kansas City & Johnson County Properties

The way a lot is laid out decides how many cars it holds, how smoothly traffic moves, and whether it works for people with disabilities. If you're opening a new lot, redoing an old one, or simply want more usable spaces, a better layout can make a real difference.

We look at your available space and design an efficient configuration that maximizes the number of spaces while keeping proper clearances, ADA accessibility, and traffic flow. Once you approve the plan, we stripe it. If your city requires an engineered site plan for larger changes, we can stripe to those plans.

What's Included

  • Reviewing your lot's dimensions and current markings
  • Designing a stall, aisle, and traffic-flow layout that maximizes capacity
  • Placing ADA stalls, access aisles, and fire lanes where they belong
  • Striping the finished layout, including stencils and directional arrows

When You Need It

  • You have a new or newly paved lot
  • You need more parking spaces without expanding
  • Traffic flow is confusing or unsafe
  • You're adding ADA stalls or fire lanes to an existing layout

How Much Does It Cost?

Layout design is quoted with your project. Striping a new layout is priced as new striping: the per-space rates plus a 75% new-layout upcharge (see the price calculator). Text us your lot's address or a photo and we'll send a price.

Parking Lot Layout Design FAQ

Can you help me fit more spaces into my lot?

Often, yes. We measure and plan a layout that maximizes the number of spaces within proper clearances and ADA requirements, then stripe it.

Do you handle ADA stalls and fire lanes in the layout?

Yes. We place accessible stalls, access aisles, and fire lanes as part of the plan so the finished lot works for everyone and keeps emergency access clear.

Do I need a site plan first?

For small changes, usually not. For larger changes your city may require an engineered plan, and if so we can stripe to it. Send us what you have and we'll tell you what we need.
