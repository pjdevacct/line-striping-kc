Parking Lot Striping for Kansas City & Johnson County Properties

Clean, well-marked parking lines do more than make a lot look good. They guide traffic, protect pedestrians, and make sure every space gets used. Line Striping KC stripes new and existing parking lots across Kansas City and Johnson County, from small church and nonprofit lots to large retail, office, and apartment properties.

We use premium water-based traffic paint made for parking lots and formulated for Kansas City's freeze-thaw cycles and summer heat. Most lots are striped in a few hours, and we can work nights and weekends so your customers and tenants are never disrupted.

What's Included

  • Cleaning the pavement so the paint bonds properly
  • Measuring and marking your layout for proper spacing and traffic flow
  • Crisp, uniform lines applied with professional striping equipment
  • Compliant ADA stalls, access aisles, and logos where needed
  • Finishing touches like fire lanes, curbs, and stencils

When You Need It

  • You're opening a new lot or have fresh asphalt or a new overlay
  • You're changing the layout or adding spaces
  • Your lines are faded, chipped, or hard to see (see re-striping)
  • You're preparing a property for new tenants, a sale, or an inspection

How Much Does It Cost?

Our calculator shows exactly how we price: $25 per space for the first 20 spaces, $20 per space for spaces 21-50, and $15 per space after 50, with a $400 minimum. A brand-new lot or full layout change adds 75%, and night or weekend work adds 25%. Try the instant price calculator or text us a photo for a firm quote.

Parking Lot Striping FAQ

How long does parking lot striping take?

Most lots take 2-4 hours. Small lots under 20 spaces often take 1-2 hours, and larger or more complex properties can take 4-8 hours. We'll give you a timeline with your quote.

How long before cars can drive on fresh paint?

Paint typically dries to the touch in 15-30 minutes under normal Kansas City weather. We recommend waiting about 1 hour before allowing full traffic. Complete curing takes about 24 hours, though this varies with temperature and humidity.

What type of paint do you use?

Premium water-based traffic paint made for parking lots. It offers excellent durability, high visibility, and fast drying, and it's formulated to withstand Kansas City's freeze-thaw cycles and summer heat.
