Warehouse Floor Marking for Kansas City & Johnson County Properties

In a warehouse or distribution center, floor markings are part of how the building runs. Clear aisle lines, walkways, and safety zones keep forklifts and pedestrians apart, speed up workflow, and support OSHA compliance. Line Striping KC provides industrial floor striping for warehouses, distribution centers, and light-industrial facilities across the Kansas City metro.

We can schedule around your operation, including nights and weekends, so your floor stays productive.

What's Included

  • Aisle and lane striping
  • Pedestrian walkways and safe zones
  • Loading dock and staging area markings
  • Hazard and equipment zone markings
  • Numbers, letters, and arrows via stencil lettering

When You Need It

  • You're opening or reconfiguring a warehouse
  • Existing floor markings are worn or unclear
  • You want to improve safety and traffic flow
  • You're preparing for a safety inspection or audit

How Much Does It Cost?

Warehouse floor marking is quoted by project. Text us photos of the space, a rough floor plan or square footage, and what you need marked, and we'll send a price.

Warehouse Floor Marking FAQ
