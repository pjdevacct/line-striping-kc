Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about parking lot striping in Kansas City

What do I need to send to get a quote?

A photo of your lot (or just the address), roughly how many spaces you have, and anything special like ADA stalls, fire lanes, stencils, or curbs. That's usually enough for us to send a price the same day.

How long does line striping take?

Most parking lots can be striped in 2-4 hours, depending on size and complexity. Small lots (under 20 spaces) often take just 1-2 hours. Larger commercial properties or complex layouts may require 4-8 hours. We'll give you a specific timeline with your quote.

How long before cars can drive on fresh paint?

Paint typically dries to the touch in 15-30 minutes under normal Kansas City weather. We recommend waiting about 1 hour before allowing full traffic. Complete curing takes about 24 hours, though this varies with temperature and humidity.

Do you offer night and weekend striping?

Yes! We frequently stripe after hours and on weekends for businesses that can't afford daytime disruption, especially retail centers, restaurants, and medical offices. Night and weekend work carries a 25% premium.

How often should a parking lot be re-striped?

In Kansas City, striping typically lasts 1-2 years depending on traffic, weather, and paint quality. High-traffic areas may need it annually. If your lines are faded or hard to see at night or in the rain, it's time. Re-striping early is more cost-effective than waiting for lines to disappear completely.

What type of paint do you use?

Premium water-based traffic paint made for parking lots. It offers excellent durability, high visibility, and fast drying, and it's formulated to withstand Kansas City's freeze-thaw cycles and summer heat.

Do you handle ADA compliance and fire lanes?

Absolutely. We handle proper handicap space dimensions, van-accessible spaces, access aisles, and International Symbol of Accessibility logos, plus fire lane markings that meet local Kansas City and Johnson County fire codes. Getting these right helps you avoid costly violations.

Can you stripe in cold weather?

Yes, with limits. We can stripe when temperatures are above 50°F and no rain is forecast for several hours. Paint doesn't adhere or dry properly in freezing conditions, so spring through fall is ideal.

Can you help design a new lot layout?

Yes! We can design a layout that maximizes your parking capacity while ensuring ADA compliance and efficient traffic flow.

Are you insured?

Yes, we carry full liability insurance for your protection and peace of mind. We're a professional, licensed contractor serving the Kansas City area.
